## Chosen Direction: Verdant Archive

### Design Movement

A contemporary **African futurist editorial identity** informed by botanical plates, archival folios, and refined civic publishing. It combines the permanence of a foundation with the energy of a living system.

### Core Principles

1. **Memory is material.** The interface should feel collected, tactile, and grounded in the physical world rather than abstract technology.
2. **The living system is the organizing structure.** Roots, trunk, branches, canopy, and circular flow should shape the layout, not just decorate it.
3. **Sovereignty is calm.** Use confident whitespace, precise typography, and measured motion instead of urgency, hype, or crisis framing.
4. **Participation is expansive.** No business domain is treated as lesser; the site should make room for farms, laboratories, retailers, builders, researchers, and cultural stewards alike.

### Color Philosophy

The palette starts from the supplied emblem: warm ivory as the ground of memory, deep leaf green as continuity and vitality, and harvest gold as the visible trace of value moving through the system. Gold is used as a signal and punctuation, not as a blanket luxury wash. Dark ink gives the page the seriousness of an archive while the green keeps it alive.

### Layout Paradigm

Use an **asymmetric editorial spine**. A persistent left-side chapter rail and offset content blocks create a sense of a long-form field journal. The hero should pair a strong statement with the emblem rather than center everything. Sections should alternate between wide, quiet reading bands and denser system diagrams.

### Signature Elements

1. Fine gold registration lines and corner marks borrowed from archival printmaking.
2. Root-like connector paths that animate between the Living Tree and Circular Flow sections.
3. Small uppercase chapter labels paired with oversized serif numerals for navigation and hierarchy.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like revealing layers of a field guide: hover states uncover context, tabs move the reader through a system, and diagrams respond with a gentle pulse rather than a loud effect. All interactive elements need clear keyboard focus and a visible sense of state.

### Animation

Use slow, organic reveals only for major sections: opacity and slight vertical movement with a 480–700ms ease-out. System nodes should draw in with staggered 40–70ms intervals. Buttons and navigation respond in under 200ms with a slight translate or scale. Avoid looping decorative motion; the living system should feel present, not restless. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing non-essential transforms.

### Typography System

Display headings use **DM Serif Display**, with large sentence-case statements and occasional uppercase chapter labels. Body and interface copy use **Manrope**, with generous line height and restrained tracking. The hierarchy is: chapter label at 11px uppercase, display headline at 64–92px on desktop, section headline at 40–56px, body at 16–18px, metadata at 12–13px.

### Brand Essence

**HAEL is a living architecture for a generation that remembers, reconnects, synchronizes, and evolves the systems that sustain us.** Personality: **rooted, lucid, generative**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are declarative and spacious. CTAs are invitations to participate, not sales commands. Microcopy should be specific, warm, and free of institutional filler.

Example lines:

> We remember what was built before us — then we return with more capability.

> Map the part you already play in the living system.

### Wordmark & Logo

Use the supplied tree-and-circuit emblem as the primary mark. Pair it with a custom-feeling HAEL wordmark treatment: high-contrast serif capitals with expanded tracking and a small diamond registration glyph used as a separator. The emblem should appear at a clearly visible size in the header and as a favicon source.

### Signature Brand Color

**Canopy Green — `#145C3A`**. A dense, ownable green that reads as both botanical and infrastructural; it should anchor interactive states, the hero field, and the living-tree diagrams.

### Content Architecture

The landing page will be organized around two simultaneous structures from the source material:

- **The Living Tree:** Roots → Trunk → Branches → Canopy.
- **The Circular Flow:** Primary → Secondary → Tertiary → Quaternary → Return → Primary.

The public narrative will move through **Remember → Root → Build → Trade → Discover → Return**, with the Foundation positioned as the trunk and public gateway.

## Style Decisions

- The page structure is governed by a left-side field-journal spine with uppercase chapter labels and oversized serif numerals; horizontal navigation supports it but does not replace it.
- African futurist specificity appears through restrained land-memory geometry, sovereign infrastructure marks, and culturally grounded pattern logic, never through generic decorative motifs.
- Gold is the trace of value moving through the system: reserve the strongest gold moments for chapter signals, key emphasis, circular-flow movement, and primary participation moments.
- Fine root-like connector paths, registration arcs, and folio lines connect the page so the scroll reads as one evolving organism.
- The HAEL wordmark should read as a distinct, repeated brand mark: high-contrast serif capitals, expanded tracking, and a diamond registration glyph should stay visible in header, section signatures, and footer.
