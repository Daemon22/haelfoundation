## Immutable identity rules

- Face and facial proportions are immutable.
- Complexion, eyes, defining features, hair, braids, and established appearance are immutable.
- SIRE's established red, gold, and green identity is preserved.
- ERIS's established violet, silver, and lunar identity is preserved.
- Signature symbols, emblems, and identity-bearing details must remain faithful to the supplied artifact.
- Clothing and environment may change only when requested.
- Pose, composition, and lighting may vary only when identity remains consistent.
- If an image operation cannot use the original as its actual visual reference, report that limitation instead of substituting another image.

## Required workflow

**Original artifact → canonical identity → controlled variation.**

Never use: **original artifact → AI reinterpretation → replacement identity.**

This rule applies to chat responses, Studio surfaces, portraits, promotional materials, codex illustrations, future scenes, and all other HAEL depictions.
